# V4.3 structural reference review

Visually revisited in Chrome on 25 September 2026. These references informed content hierarchy and page depth, not Oxidara formulas, claims, directions, branding or wording.

- [Rare Beauty](https://www.rarebeauty.com/): warm product-focused hero, consistent studio product row, then human beauty editorial within a coherent light environment. Applied as product discovery followed by the new upper-homepage human moment.
- [Rhode](https://www.rhodeskin.com/) and [Glazing Milk](https://www.rhodeskin.com/products/glazing-milk): large photography, concise product introduction, then distinct application and ingredient modules. Oxidara uses this readable sequence, with conspicuous illustrative-content notices. Rhode's product formula, clinical numbers and before/after imagery were not transferred.
- [Makeup by Mario](https://www.makeupbymario.com/) and its [cream eyeshadow PDP](https://www.makeupbymario.com/products/soft-shimmer-long-wear-cream-eyeshadow): clear gallery/information split, named color choices and progressively detailed product information. Oxidara retains its existing PDP structure and adds explicitly proposed wardrobe palette chips.

The V4.2 ivory canvas, main navigation, seven-product catalog, default duo hero, restrained motion and functional safeguards remain the foundation. V4.3 adds depth rather than replacing that design.
