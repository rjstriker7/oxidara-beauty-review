# Oxidara V4.3 — completed / open

25 September 2026. Focused V4.2 refinement. **Public approval preview.** V4.3 replaces V4.2 at the existing separate GitHub Pages review link following the owner’s sharing request. No store launch, accounts, hosting-provider, brand-domain or DNS changes.

## Completed

- Preserved the ivory environment, default Mist/Melt duo hero, seven Coming Soon products, brand-wide signup and disabled demo forms. No prices, cart or checkout. Purifying Oil remains separate, with its use type unresolved.
- Added an upper-homepage portrait of a smiling adult woman holding the Mist, visibly labeled as a generated campaign concept, not product-results evidence.
- Grouped men’s and women’s wardrobe in a parallel capsule. Two new active women’s images use athletic styling and mineral outdoor settings. The proposed tops remain outside the catalog.
- Added Olive, Ink, Stone and Red Earth palette proposals for men and women. Accessible chips preview a tone, not a rendered garment variant or purchasable option.
- Expanded Collection with formats, ingredient education and application-reading guidance.
- Populated all five beauty PDPs with distinct overviews and three-step example guidance. Four liquids have 3–4 named illustrative ingredient profiles each. Mask uses preparation, handling and care without invented technology or wear time.
- Expanded Science into six sections: proposed principles, eight-entry ingredient library, general cosmetic roles and final-product information. The texture image is explicitly illustrative.
- Expanded Story around Beauty Beyond Earth, faith, fire, restoration and Prepare. Release. Renew. An imagined red-earth landscape restores the Mars influence; no founder history was invented.
- Applied changes to shared Shopify Liquid and rebuilt root/subpath static snapshots.
