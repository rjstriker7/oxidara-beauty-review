Care in the details.

There is more to an accessory than its first impression. Getting familiar with its shape, knowing how to handle it and giving it a considered place to rest are part of caring for the object itself.

Explore the mask and its packaging in the gallery, then take a moment with the preparation, handling and care guide below.

Caring for your accessory

Preparation,
handling & care.

Begin with the product guide, handle with care and keep the mask ready for its next use. An accessory deserves its own approach.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    Read the supplied product guide and inspect the mask before use. Begin with clean hands and a clear, clean place to set it down.

  2. 02

    Handling

    Handle gently and follow the guide for placement and removal. Keep the eye and breathing areas unobstructed, and avoid pulling or altering the mask.

  3. 03

    Care

    Follow the product guide for cleaning and storage. Use the method suited to its materials rather than choosing a cleaning liquid or temperature treatment by appearance.
