Ingredient spotlights

A closer look at hydration.

Meet three familiar ingredients used in moisture-focused skincare. These spotlights explain the language of hydration, from binding water to conditioning the feel of skin.

From our ingredient library: general skincare education, separate from this product’s ingredient list.

1Hydration

Hyaluronic acid

A humectant: an ingredient that binds water. In skincare, hyaluronic acid is used to help the surface of skin feel hydrated and supple. Its role depends on the complete formula.

2Hydration

Glycerin

A familiar moisture-binding ingredient found across skincare formats. Glycerin helps retain water in a formula and contributes to a comfortable, hydrated feel on skin.

3Conditioning

Panthenol

Also known as provitamin B5, panthenol is used in cosmetic formulas for its skin-conditioning and moisture-retaining properties. It is one example of an ingredient chosen with skin feel in mind.

The application gesture

A moment,
step by step.

A light touch, a moment to pause. Begin with clean hands and follow the product label for application areas and precautions.

  1. 01

    Begin with a clean canvas

    Start with clean hands and skin. Keep the bottle upright and prepare to use the spray away from your eyes.

  2. 02

    Mist gently

    Close your eyes and lips, then gently mist the face. Keep the spray out of the eyes and follow the label for the intended application area.

  3. 03

    Let it settle

    Allow the mist to settle, then close the bottle. Any next product should be chosen according to its own directions and your preferences.
