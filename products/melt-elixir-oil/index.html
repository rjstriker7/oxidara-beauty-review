Ingredient spotlights

Understanding the oil format.

Emollients and oil-compatible ingredients help shape how a cosmetic oil feels. Explore three different roles, from glide and skin conditioning to antioxidants within a formula.

From our ingredient library: general skincare education, separate from this product’s ingredient list.

1Conditioning

Squalane

An emollient used to soften and smooth the feel of skin. This clear oil-like ingredient appears in many cosmetic textures; its presence alone does not determine a finished product’s richness or finish.

2Conditioning oils

Jojoba oil

Derived from jojoba seeds, this ingredient is technically a liquid wax. In skincare it is used as an emollient, contributing to glide and a soft, smooth skin feel.

3Antioxidants

Vitamin E

Tocopherol is a form of vitamin E used in cosmetics as an antioxidant and skin-conditioning ingredient. Antioxidants can help limit oxidation within a formula; that does not establish a finished product’s performance on skin.

The application gesture

A moment,
step by step.

Keep the application simple and the touch gentle. The product label is the guide for where to apply and which precautions to follow.

  1. 01

    Prepare with care

    With clean hands, dispense the oil into your palm. Keep the dropper opening clear of the face and other surfaces.

  2. 02

    Smooth softly

    Gently smooth over the face, avoiding the eye area. Let a light touch guide the gesture rather than rubbing or pulling at the skin.

  3. 03

    Finish at your own pace

    Allow the oil to settle and close the bottle after use. Follow each product’s own directions when choosing what comes before or after.
