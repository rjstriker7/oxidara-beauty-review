Ingredient spotlights

The language of a serum.

A serum is a format with many possible compositions. These ingredient spotlights introduce four familiar names and the different roles they can play in skincare.

From our ingredient library: general skincare education, separate from this product’s ingredient list.

1Skin texture

Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 used in skincare, including formulas concerned with the look and feel of skin texture. The ingredient’s role should be understood in the context of the whole formula.

2Hydration

Hyaluronic acid

A humectant: an ingredient that binds water. In skincare, hyaluronic acid is used to help the surface of skin feel hydrated and supple. Its role depends on the complete formula.

3Barrier support

Ceramides

A family of lipids also found in the skin’s outer layer. Cosmetic ceramides, such as ceramide NP, are used in formulas designed around moisture retention and skin conditioning.

4Skin conditioning

Peptides

Peptides are chains of amino acids. They are a broad ingredient family, with different structures and cosmetic roles; a specific peptide needs its own explanation rather than a promise attached to the family name.

The application gesture

A moment,
step by step.

A considered approach starts with clean hands and a gentle gesture. Read the product label before adding a serum to your routine.

  1. 01

    Get ready

    Dispense the serum into a clean hand. Avoid touching the applicator directly to the face, and keep the bottle opening clean.

  2. 02

    Press with a light touch

    Gently spread or press onto the face with your fingertips, avoiding the eye area. Keep the movement soft and follow the label for application areas.

  3. 03

    Give it a moment

    Let the serum settle before choosing another product. Close the bottle after use and follow its storage guidance.
