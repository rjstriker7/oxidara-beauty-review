Ingredient spotlights · Preview

The language of a serum.

A serum is a format, rather than a single ingredient or a promise of results. These four example profiles offer a useful starting point for reading skincare labels; they do not represent this serum’s composition.

Illustrative preview content. Ingredients and directions are not confirmed for this product.

1Skin texture

Example ingredient profile

Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 used in skincare, including formulas concerned with the look and feel of skin texture. The ingredient’s role should be understood in the context of the whole formula.

2Hydration

Example ingredient profile

Hyaluronic acid

A humectant: an ingredient that binds water. In skincare, hyaluronic acid is used to help the surface of skin feel hydrated and supple. Its role depends on the complete formula.

3Barrier support

Example ingredient profile

Ceramides

A family of lipids also found in the skin’s outer layer. Cosmetic ceramides, such as ceramide NP, are used in formulas designed around moisture retention and skin conditioning.

4Skin conditioning

Example ingredient profile

Peptides

Peptides are chains of amino acids. They are a broad ingredient family, with different structures and cosmetic roles; a specific peptide needs its own explanation rather than a promise attached to the family name.

How to apply.
Example guidance.

A complete draft for this product format, prepared for owner review. These are examples, not approved instructions for an Oxidara product.

Illustrative preview content. Ingredients and directions are not confirmed for this product.

  1. 01

    Get ready

    Begin with clean hands and review the final label. Dispense into your hand without allowing the applicator to touch the face.

  2. 02

    Apply with a light touch

    For this draft serum example, spread gently across the face, avoiding the eye area. Application areas and compatibility must follow the approved product guidance.

  3. 03

    Keep the routine considered

    Let the serum settle before deciding on any other product. Close the bottle after use and follow its finalized storage guidance.
