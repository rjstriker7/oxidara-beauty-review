Ingredient education

Familiar names.
A fuller understanding.

Humectants, emollients, lipids: a little vocabulary makes skincare easier to read. Our ingredient library explores these general cosmetic roles, separately from individual product formulas.

01Hydration

Hyaluronic acid

A humectant: an ingredient that binds water. In skincare, hyaluronic acid is used to help the surface of skin feel hydrated and supple. Its role depends on the complete formula.

02Conditioning

Squalane

An emollient used to soften and smooth the feel of skin. This clear oil-like ingredient appears in many cosmetic textures; its presence alone does not determine a finished product’s richness or finish.

03Barrier support

Ceramides

A family of lipids also found in the skin’s outer layer. Cosmetic ceramides, such as ceramide NP, are used in formulas designed around moisture retention and skin conditioning.

Your approach to application

Choose with intention.
Keep it personal.

A good starting point is understanding what you reach for and why. You can explore a collection without treating it as a sequence or using every product together.

  1. 01

    Get to know the format

    Begin with the product overview. An oil and a serum can look similar in a bottle while serving different purposes and needing different guidance.

  2. 02

    Understand the gesture

    Read the application or handling guide on the product page. The method should belong to the specific product, rather than a familiar name or packaging style.

  3. 03

    Read the label together

    Look at ingredients, directions and precautions as a whole. Your preferences and the product’s own guidance are a more useful starting point than a fixed number of steps.
